Privacy Policy

Effective May 28, 2026 · Updated May 28, 2026

Twirl ("Twirl," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Twirl LLC (Arkansas LLC — registration pending), provides a peer-to-peer clothing rental marketplace (the "Service") through our mobile app and website at twirl.rentals. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Service.

1. Who this applies to

The Service is intended only for users who are 18 years of age or older and affiliated with a participating university. By using Twirl you confirm you meet these requirements.

2. Information we collect

You provide directly

  • Account & identity: name, email address (we verify a .edu school email), password (stored hashed by our authentication provider).
  • Profile & demographics: university/school, sorority/affiliation, profile photo, and other details you choose to add.
  • Listings & content: photos and descriptions of items you list, messages you send to other users, reviews, reports, and rental/contract details.

Collected automatically

  • Device & usage data: app version, device type, OS, and basic interaction/diagnostic data used to operate and improve the Service and to detect abuse.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Twirl does not collect or store your full card number. Card data is sent directly to Stripe. We store limited payment metadata (e.g., a Stripe customer/account identifier, payment status, payout status, amounts, and security-deposit hold status) needed to operate rentals, commissions, and payouts. See Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account and verify school eligibility.
  • Operate the marketplace: list items, request/approve rentals, sign rental contracts, message, review, and resolve disputes.
  • Process payments, the 15% platform commission, owner payouts, and security-deposit holds and releases via Stripe.
  • Provide safety and trust features: reporting, blocking, and content moderation.
  • Send service-related notifications (e.g., a rental request, approval, payment, message).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and policy or legal violations.
  • Improve and maintain the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How we share information

  • With other users: your public profile (name, photo, school/affiliation, reviews) and your listings are visible to other users. When you transact, the counterparty sees information necessary to complete the rental (e.g., first name, messages, rental details).
  • Service providers: Stripe (payments/payouts), Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage hosting), and our app/build and analytics/crash-reporting providers, each only as needed to run the Service.
  • Legal & safety: when required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of users or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal/tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When you delete your account (see Section 7), we delete or anonymize your personal information, except records we must retain for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes (e.g., transaction and payout records).

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access/update: edit your profile in the app.
  • Notifications: control push notifications in your device settings.
  • Depending on your state of residence (including California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them. To make a request, contact us at support@twirl.rentals.

7. Account deletion

You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time, in the app, via Profile → Delete Account. Deletion removes or anonymizes your profile, listings, saved items, and messages. We retain transaction, payout, and dispute records as required for legal and financial compliance, in de-identified form where possible. You can also request deletion by emailing support@twirl.rentals.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, database row-level security, and a PCI-compliant payment processor (Stripe). No system is perfectly secure; use a strong, unique password.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
